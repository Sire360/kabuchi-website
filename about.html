Who we are

Where Beauty Meets Culture

Kabuchi Spa & Beauty was created to give Lusaka a beauty space that feels both luxurious and welcoming. Here you can unwind with a massage, refresh your nails, transform your hair or get glam for a special day, all under one roof.

We believe beauty is for everyone. Our team celebrates our diverse cultures and works with every skin tone and hair type, using quality products and professional techniques, with genuine care in every detail.

  • Nails, hair, make-up & spa
  • Inclusive for everyone
  • Trained, friendly team
  • Open every day

Vision & Mission

Our Vision
